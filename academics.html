ACADEMICS

A curriculum built for every stage

HBSE-aligned teaching from Pre-Primary through Class X, delivered by experienced faculty and supported by regular assessment and individual attention.

Stages of Learning

From first steps to board exams

I.

Pre-Primary (Nursery – KG)

A play-based, joyful introduction to school life focused on language development, motor skills, social skills, and early number sense.

II.

Primary (Class I – V)

Core subjects — English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS/Science — taught through activity-based learning, with growing focus on reading and writing skills.

III.

Middle School (Class VI – VIII)

Introduction to Science, Social Science, and Computer Applications alongside core subjects, building analytical and independent-study skills.

IV.

Secondary (Class IX – X)

Focused, structured preparation for the HBSE Class X board examinations, with regular tests, doubt-clearing sessions, and revision support.

Subjects Offered

A well-rounded core curriculum
