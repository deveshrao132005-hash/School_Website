ABOUT US

Our story, values, and people

RBS Public School has been a part of the Jaitpur and Rewari community, shaping students through a blend of academic rigour and strong personal values.

Our History

Nurturing Minds, Building Futures

RBS Public School was established in 2003 with a simple mission: to provide quality, accessible education to the children of Jaitpur and the wider Rewari region. Since then, it has grown into a full HBSE-affiliated institution serving Nursery through Class X.

Our focus has always stayed close to two things: a genuine emphasis on study, and equal attention to a student's personal development — through everyday encouragement, warm greetings, and play and activity time that every student takes part in. That, alongside GPS-enabled transport, experienced staff, and a disciplined school culture, is what we've built over the years.

Our Foundation

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a school where every child from Rural area discovers their potential and grows into a confident, responsible, and capable individual, ready to contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To keep study at the centre of school life while giving equal weight to each student's personal development — through experienced faculty, disciplined routines, and a school that genuinely listens to parents and acts on their feedback.

Leadership

Principal's Message

"Our mission is to build a school where every child learns with freedom, grows with values, and walks forward with confidence. We believe that education is not just about books, but about learning the art of life - with discipline, cleanliness, morality, and innovation. Our dream is to see every child from our village become a bright and responsible citizen of the nation."
